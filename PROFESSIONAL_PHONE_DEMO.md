# Five-Minute Professional Phone Demonstration

## Before the conversation

- Open Handiwave in a mobile browser and keep a sample professional account ready.
- Prepare one sample electrical or plumbing request in the Barnawa–Narayi corridor.
- Keep mobile data on and use the applicant's own phone for the final Home Screen step.
- Do not use real customer contact, address, payment, or identity data in the demonstration.

## Demonstration script

### 0:00–0:40 — Set expectations

> Handiwave helps approved professionals receive suitable service opportunities and keep clear job records. Registration is free. We do not promise a fixed number of jobs. Let me show you the full process in five minutes.

Show the professional profile and point out trade, service area, work samples, verification labels, and reviews.

### 0:40–1:40 — Receive and inspect a request

Open the Jobs screen. Show the service type, broad location, requested time, customer description, and status. Explain that the professional should accept only work they can complete safely and on time.

Say:

> Read the request before accepting. If information is missing, ask through Handiwave. Do not claim a skill you do not have.

### 1:40–2:40 — Send a clear quote

Show the quote action and explain the expected breakdown:

- Inspection or call-out fee.
- Labour.
- Materials and who buys them.
- Expected completion time.
- Anything excluded from the quote.

Say:

> Do not hide charges. If the scope changes, explain it and get approval before continuing.

### 2:40–3:35 — Update the job

Show accepted, in-progress, and completion states. Demonstrate a customer update and explain arrival, delay, and completion evidence.

Say:

> Keep the customer informed. Upload only relevant work evidence and protect private information.

### 3:35–4:20 — Payment and problems

Show the wallet or payment record without exposing real financial data. Explain that payout follows the pilot payment process. Point out messages and dispute support.

Say:

> Keep agreements and updates inside Handiwave. If scope, safety, payment, or customer behaviour becomes a problem, pause and contact support.

### 4:20–5:00 — Add to Home Screen

On Android Chrome: menu → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

On iPhone Safari: Share → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

Ask the applicant to reopen Handiwave from the new icon, locate Jobs, and explain the next action on the sample request. Mark the product check as passed only if they can repeat the basic flow.

## Recruiter close

> Your profile becomes active only after the required checks pass. Opportunities depend on real customer demand and your trade, location, response, and performance. What part would you like me to show again?
