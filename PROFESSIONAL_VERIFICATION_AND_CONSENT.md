# Professional Verification and Consent Pack

## Recruiter checklist

Record each result as `Pending`, `Passed`, `Needs review`, or `Failed`. Never award a badge from profile completion alone.

| Check | Evidence to collect | Pass standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact | Working phone number and email | Applicant responds and controls both channels |
| Identity | Government-issued ID and live selfie | Name/photo reasonably match; document is legible and current where applicable |
| Address | Home, workshop, or operating address | Address is recorded and can be located or corroborated |
| Trade | Primary and secondary services | Services are specific and consistent with work evidence |
| Experience | Years active and work history | Explanation is coherent and references can support it |
| References | Two previous customers | Both can be contacted; at least one confirms satisfactory work |
| Work samples | Three recent photos or videos | Applicant explains what they personally completed |
| Commercial | Inspection fee, price range, and materials practice | Information is clear enough to quote honestly |
| Coverage | Barnawa–Narayi availability and response time | Applicant can reliably serve the pilot corridor |
| Payment | Bank-account name | Name matches applicant or difference is documented |
| Technical | Practical check or qualified reference for higher-risk work | Reviewer is satisfied the applicant can perform the listed service safely |
| Conduct | Pilot rules and customer-care briefing | Applicant accepts punctuality, updates, clean-up, and dispute process |
| Product | Phone demonstration | Applicant can accept, quote, update, and close a sample job |

## Badge wording

- **Identity checked:** Handiwave reviewed an identity document, a matching live selfie, and control of the registered phone number.
- **References checked:** Handiwave contacted at least two submitted references and recorded the outcome.
- **Skill checked:** Handiwave reviewed a practical demonstration, recognised evidence, or a qualified technical reference for the named trade.
- **Job-proven:** The professional completed at least three Handiwave jobs without an unresolved quality or conduct issue.

Do not replace these labels with a general “certified” claim. Identity checking is not a technical qualification.

## Required applicant consent

> I confirm that my application information is accurate. I consent to Handiwave collecting and using my contact details, identity documents, location, work samples, references, and verification records to assess my application, create and manage my professional profile, prevent fraud, and contact me about the pilot and service opportunities. I understand that verification does not guarantee jobs and may be withdrawn if information is false. I agree to the Handiwave Terms and Privacy Policy.

Record the consent version, acceptance date and time, account ID, and recruitment source. The website uses consent version `kaduna-pilot-v1-2026-10-07`.

## Reference call consent

Before calling a reference, ask the applicant:

> Do you confirm that these people agreed to be contacted by Handiwave about work you completed for them?

Opening line for the reference:

> Hello, my name is [name] from Handiwave. [Applicant] gave us your contact as a previous customer and said you agreed to a short reference check. Is now a suitable time, and may we record your answers in the applicant's verification file?

## Work-photo consent

> I confirm that I took or am authorised to share these work samples. I consent to Handiwave using approved samples on my public professional profile and in service-related promotion. I will not submit customer faces, addresses, documents, or other private information without permission.

## Verification outcome wording

Approved:

> Your Handiwave application has passed the checks listed in your profile. Approval gives access to suitable pilot opportunities when available; it does not guarantee a minimum number or value of jobs.

Needs review:

> We need additional information before your profile can become active: [items]. Your application remains inactive until these checks are completed.

Not approved:

> Handiwave cannot activate your application at this time because [plain-language reason]. You may request correction of inaccurate information or reapply when [condition] is resolved.
