# Handiwave Kaduna Artisan-First Launch Plan

## 1. The Honest Answer: Can a Website Perform Like an App?

No strategy can guarantee activity. A native app also does not guarantee that people will return, accept jobs, or pay for services.

Handiwave can, however, deliver the important parts of an app experience before paying for app-store publishing:

- Customers and professionals can install Handiwave on their phone home screen.
- The installed website can open in its own app-like window.
- Professionals can receive job alerts through web notifications after that feature is completed.
- WhatsApp and SMS can back up urgent alerts so opportunities are not missed.
- A human operations line can help users complete bookings while the marketplace is still young.

The real activity loop is not "download an app." It is:

1. A real customer requests a service.
2. A suitable professional receives the request quickly.
3. The professional responds with a clear quote.
4. Handiwave helps both parties complete the job safely.
5. The professional is paid and the customer leaves a review.
6. Both have a reason to return.

The website will feel active when that loop works reliably.

## 2. Recommended Kaduna Launch Position

Launch Handiwave as:

> Kaduna's managed network for finding dependable local professionals and helping skilled workers receive serious service requests.

Do not launch across all of Kaduna at once. Begin with one area cluster that the founder can physically supervise. Two reasonable pilot options are:

- Kaduna South: Barnawa, Narayi, Sabon Tasha and nearby communities.
- Kaduna North: Malali, Ungwan Rimi and nearby communities.

Choose the cluster where the founder already has the strongest personal, business, estate, religious and community connections.

## 3. The Founding 20 Professionals

The first goal is not hundreds of registrations. The first goal is 20 responsive professionals who can complete jobs well.

### Suggested Mix

| Category | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Electrical and solar/inverter | 5 |
| Plumbing | 4 |
| AC and refrigeration | 4 |
| Generator repair | 3 |
| Cleaning teams | 2 |
| Carpentry and general handyman work | 2 |
| **Total** | **20** |

Adjust this mix after the first 20 customer requests reveal the strongest demand.

### Founding Professional Offer

- Free registration and profile setup.
- No payment simply to receive or view a lead during the pilot.
- Handiwave earns only when a paid job is completed.
- Help creating a strong profile with service photos and clear pricing.
- Early access to Kaduna service requests.
- Founding Professional recognition inside the platform.
- Fast support when a customer or job issue occurs.

Do not promise guaranteed jobs. Say "access to service opportunities" or "receive service requests when available."

## 4. Where to Recruit in Kaduna

### Priority 1: Old Panteka, Tudun Wada

Old Panteka is the strongest broad artisan recruitment point. Kaduna State and Kaduna South sources describe a very large concentration of workers in welding, fabrication, carpentry, masonry, mechanical and related trades.

Approach association leaders and trade-section leaders before distributing flyers. Ask each leader to nominate three to five dependable members rather than signing up everyone immediately.

### Priority 2: KIVTSD Rigachikun

The Kaduna State Institute of Vocational Training and Skills Development is a strong source of newly trained professionals in electrical installation, welding, AC and refrigeration, plumbing, solar, carpentry, painting, tiling and other trades.

Ask for permission to present Handiwave to graduates or collect applications from graduates who already have tools and can travel to customer locations.

### Priority 3: Trade-Supply Shops

Visit electrical, plumbing, AC, generator, solar and building-material shops. Shop owners know which artisans buy materials regularly, pay reliably and receive repeat referrals.

Offer each shop a simple referral code. Track how many approved professionals and completed jobs came from each shop.

### Priority 4: Existing Community Networks

- Estate and residents' associations.
- Churches and mosques.
- Landlords and facility caretakers.
- WhatsApp trade and neighbourhood groups.
- Previous customers of trusted artisans.

## 5. Professional Approval Checklist

An applicant should not receive a trust badge merely for creating an account. Use a clear approval process:

- Government-issued ID.
- Live selfie matched to the applicant.
- Verified phone number.
- Home, workshop or operating address.
- Two previous customer references.
- Three recent work samples.
- Primary trade and service area.
- Typical inspection fee and price range.
- Bank-account name matched to the applicant where possible.
- Association, employer or trainer reference where available.
- Optional NSQ, NBTE, KIVTSD or other training evidence.
- Short practical or technical reference check for higher-risk services.

Define every badge precisely. For example, "Identity Checked" should not be presented as "Skill Certified."

## 6. Field Recruitment Funnel

Every flyer, representative and partner location should have its own tracked link or code.

1. Professional scans the QR code or opens the Kaduna join link.
2. The Professional role is already selected.
3. The applicant enters name, phone, trade and location.
4. A Handiwave representative schedules or completes verification.
5. Profile photos, work samples, coverage area and pricing are added.
6. The professional learns how to accept, quote and update a job.
7. Handiwave is added to the phone's home screen.
8. The professional becomes active only after completing the checklist.

Recommended dedicated link:

`handiwave.com.ng/join-professionals`

Until that page exists, use the shortest existing professional-signup link and preselect the Professional role.

## 7. Making the Website Active Like an App

### Already Available

- Home-screen installation support.
- Standalone app-like display.
- Offline shell for basic loading.
- In-platform messages and notifications.
- Professional onboarding and service profiles.

### Highest-Priority Improvements

1. **Background job notifications:** send web push alerts for new requests, messages, quote updates and payment events.
2. **WhatsApp/SMS backup:** send a short fallback alert when a time-sensitive request is not opened quickly.
3. **Kaduna professional landing page:** explain the offer, requirements and next step in one screen.
4. **One-tap installation help:** show Android installation and iPhone "Add to Home Screen" instructions during onboarding.
5. **Availability control:** let professionals mark themselves Available, Busy or Offline.
6. **Daily opportunity digest:** send one controlled summary rather than excessive notifications.
7. **Low-data experience:** compress images, save unfinished forms and avoid repeated downloads.
8. **Source tracking:** measure every QR code, partner and field representative.
9. **Operations dashboard:** display open requests, unresponsive professionals, late quotes and jobs needing support.

Apple documents that Home Screen web apps can use Web Push on supported iPhones without requiring the website owner to join the Apple Developer Program. This makes web notifications a practical bridge before native publishing.

## 8. Contract Acquisition Strategy

Do not wait one full week after recruiting professionals before looking for jobs. Start demand outreach two or three days after the first dependable professionals are approved.

### First Contract Targets

- Estate and property managers.
- Shortlets and small hotels.
- Schools and training centres.
- Clinics and pharmacies.
- Churches and mosques.
- Small offices, shops and restaurants.
- Landlords with multiple properties.

### Offer: Kaduna Maintenance Partner Pilot

Handiwave provides:

- One WhatsApp and website request point.
- Access to checked local professionals.
- Itemised quotes before work begins.
- Job photos and completion records.
- One record for each service request.
- Support when work is delayed or disputed.
- Agreed response expectations for urgent and normal requests.

Ask for a 30-day preferred-provider pilot, not a large annual contract. The first objective is to prove response speed, work quality and reporting.

### Founder Outreach Target

| Funnel Stage | 30-Day Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Organisations contacted | 20 |
| Decision-maker meetings | 8 |
| Pilot organisations | 3 |
| Real paid jobs | 10+ |
| Customer-approved case studies | 3 |

### Early Revenue Model

- Use a transparent service fee on completed work.
- Allow a clear inspection or diagnostic fee where appropriate.
- Do not charge professionals for raw leads during the launch.
- Do not introduce subscriptions until professionals consistently receive value.
- Describe payments as protected only when the actual payment and dispute process supports that claim.

## 9. Thirty-Day Execution Plan

### Days 1–3: Prepare

- Finalise the professional flyer and tracked Kaduna join link.
- Create the professional landing page or simplify the existing signup path.
- Prepare the verification checklist and consent wording.
- Create a spreadsheet or dashboard for the recruitment funnel.
- Prepare a short professional demonstration on a phone.
- Select the single pilot area cluster.

### Days 4–7: Recruit the First 10

- Meet Old Panteka trade or association leaders.
- Visit relevant trade-supply shops.
- Contact KIVTSD Rigachikun about graduate recruitment.
- Interview applicants in small batches.
- Approve only people who meet the checklist.
- Install Handiwave on every approved professional's phone.

### Week 2: Reach 20 and Start Demand

- Complete the Founding 20 cohort.
- Run a controlled test request for every category.
- Contact 20 contract prospects.
- Begin customer outreach inside the chosen pilot area.
- Assign a human operator to watch every open request.

### Week 3: Complete Real Jobs

- Focus on ten paid jobs rather than traffic alone.
- Call customers after service completion.
- Resolve poor outcomes immediately.
- Collect permission for reviews, photos and case studies.
- Suspend professionals who repeatedly ignore requests.

### Week 4: Decide What Scales

- Keep categories with strong demand and good completion rates.
- Pause categories that attract registrations but few jobs.
- Promote the highest-performing professionals.
- Present pilot results to more estates and organisations.
- Decide whether to expand to a second Kaduna area cluster.

## 10. Daily Operations During the Pilot

- 8:00 a.m.: confirm which professionals are available.
- Every 15 minutes: review unanswered new requests during working hours.
- Within 10 minutes: contact a customer if their request is unclear.
- Within 15 minutes: escalate a request if the first professional does not respond.
- After each job: confirm completion separately with customer and professional.
- End of day: review open jobs, disputes, payments and next-day availability.

This human-assisted process is essential at the beginning. Automation should replace repeated work only after the operating pattern is proven.

## 11. Success Measures

### Professional Funnel

- Flyer/link visits.
- Started registrations.
- Completed profiles.
- Approved professionals.
- Professionals active in the last seven days.
- Median time to respond to a request.
- Quote acceptance rate.
- First completed job rate.
- Repeat-job rate.

### Marketplace Health

- Customer requests received.
- Requests receiving a quote.
- Paid jobs completed.
- Cancellation and complaint rates.
- Average time from request to accepted professional.
- Customer rating and recommendation intent.
- Revenue per completed job.

The main launch number is **customer-confirmed paid jobs completed**, not downloads, registrations or social-media views.

We would like to run a 30-day preferred-provider pilot for selected maintenance requests, with no long-term commitment.
