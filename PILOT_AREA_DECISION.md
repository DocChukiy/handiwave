# Kaduna Pilot Area Decision

## Selected cluster

**Barnawa–Narayi corridor, Kaduna South**

Initial coverage means Barnawa, Narayi, and immediately adjacent communities that a professional can reach reliably. Sabon Tasha is not part of the first service promise; it can be added only after response time and completion quality are proven.

## Why this cluster

- It is a tighter, more manageable residential and small-business service cell than a city-wide launch.
- It is close enough to recruit supply through Old Panteka and Kaduna South trade networks without treating the recruitment market as the customer-service boundary.
- The mix of homes, estates, shops, schools, worship centres, and small offices can produce repeat electrical, plumbing, AC, generator, cleaning, and handyman demand.
- A founder or field representative can physically supervise verification and early jobs.

## Pilot boundaries

- Public promise: service availability in the Barnawa–Narayi corridor only.
- Professional eligibility: must reliably travel within the corridor and state a realistic response window.
- Initial supply: 20 approved professionals, not 20 unverified registrations.
- Initial services: electrical/solar, plumbing, AC/refrigeration, generator repair, cleaning, carpentry, and general handyman work.
- Expansion trigger: at least 15 completed paid jobs, 80% completion without replacement, median first response under 15 minutes during operating hours, and no unresolved safety incident.

## Recruitment source versus service area

Old Panteka remains the first broad recruitment point. It is not the advertised pilot service area. Recruiters should prioritise professionals from any source who can meet the Barnawa–Narayi response and verification requirements.
